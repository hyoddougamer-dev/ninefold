剪 The cut

One file arrives and the game needs 166 of them, so npm run slice cuts the sheet up. The whole difficulty is finding the rules, and it took three tries. The first cutter looked for the flattest rows of pixels and settled on bare paper eight pixels off the line. The second looked for the darkest rows, and a dark creature leaning on the edge of its panel is darker than a pencil line. A rule is not the darkest thing on a page. It is the thinnest: a row much darker than the paper three to twenty pixels either side of it, and nothing else on a painted sheet is thin in that way. The third cutter measures that contrast, hunts a third of a cell around where each rule ought to be, and falls back to the nominal line when it finds nothing convincing.
