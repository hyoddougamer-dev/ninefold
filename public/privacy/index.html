九境Ninefold · Privacy

Last changed 26 September 2026.

The short version

Ninefold keeps your game on your own device. Nothing about you leaves it unless you choose to join the rankings, and even then only what the rankings need. There are no ads, no analytics and no trackers, and nothing is ever sold or shared.

On your device

Your save (the state of your cultivator) is kept in your browser's or the app's own storage on your device. It is not sent anywhere while you play without the rankings. Clearing the site's data or removing the app removes it.

If you join the rankings

The rankings run on Supabase, hosted in the European Union (Paris). When you join, it stores:

  • An account. A random id. If you play as a guest, that is all. If you sign in by email, your email address, used only to send you sign-in links.
  • The name you choose, shown on the boards.
  • Your save and the times you synced, so the game can offer your cultivator on another device and check that every climb on the boards happened in real time.
  • A record of each sync (when, and whether it passed the checks), kept to find cheating.

Nothing else: no location, no contacts, no device identifiers, no payment details.

Deleting it

In the game, open Menu → Rankings → Delete my ranked account. Your account, name, place on the boards, cloud copy and sync records are removed from the server at once. The game on your device is not touched.

Who else is involved

  • Supabase stores the ranked data described above.
  • GitHub Pages serves the game's files. Like any web server, it may keep ordinary access logs.

Children

Ninefold is not directed at children under 13, and the rankings do not knowingly collect anything from them.

Questions
